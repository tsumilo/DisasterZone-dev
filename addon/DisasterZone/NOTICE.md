# DisasterZone: third-party notices

DisasterZone 0.5.0 ships two kinds of third-party material:

- open-source libraries, in `Libs\`
- item data that was generated from other addons' data, in `Data\`

Everything below was checked against the files in this folder and the installed source addons on 2026-09-28.
Keep this file, and the license files inside `Libs\`, whenever you pass DisasterZone on.

This file is a list of attributions and known license terms. It is not legal advice.

## 2. Generated item data (`Data\`)

These six files are generated by scripts, which are not included in the addon. Each file's header lists its exact inputs.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Data\Items.lua` | Raids, bosses and their drops; tier token → piece tables, token classes, tier-piece classes, Sunmote upgrades and arena exchanges |
| `Data\Dungeons.lua` | TBC 5-man dungeons, bosses, normal and heroic drops |
| `Data\Sources.lua` | Crafted gear (by profession and specialisation), reputation rewards, Badge of Justice vendor items and PvP gear |
| `Data\ItemClasses.lua` (new in 0.5.0) | Class-restricted items (910 item IDs) and the classes that may use them, for example T5 class trinkets, Warglaives, PvP set pieces and Ashtongue talismans. Tier pieces and tokens aren't listed here, because `Items.lua` already carries their classes. |

| `Data\Atlas.lua` (new in 1.3.0) | The Atlas tab's browse lists beyond raids and dungeons: every TBC profession recipe list (enchants as spell IDs), every reputation reward by standing, PvP rewards, the Badge of Justice and Sunmote vendors, world epics, mounts, companions, tabards, legendaries, world event rewards and the item sets (Tier 4-6, arena, honor). Plus the English names of every item the tab can show, for its search. |
| `Data\BiSLists.lua` (new in 1.2.0) | The suggested BiS lists of My BiS > Suggest: per class guide and phase 1-5, item IDs with their slot and a "BIS" mark. Taken from LoonBestInSlot's guide files; see "LoonBestInSlot BiS lists" below. |

### Main source: AtlasLootClassic (GNU GPL v2)

`Items.lua`, `Dungeons.lua`, `Sources.lua`, `ItemClasses.lua` and `Atlas.lua` are derived mainly from **AtlasLootClassic** by Okrah and Lag, version v2.5.6.12382 (the copy installed in `Interface\AddOns`).

**License, checked in the installed files:**

- `AtlasLootClassic\LICENSE` is the full text of the **GNU General Public License, Version 2, June 1991**.
- Every AtlasLootClassic module used declares `## X-License: GPL v2` in its TOC: Core, DungeonsAndRaids, Crafting, Factions, Collections, PvP and Data.

**AtlasLootClassic files used:**

- `AtlasLootClassic_DungeonsAndRaids\data-tbc.lua`: raids, dungeons, bosses, drops per difficulty, NPC and object IDs, content phases, item-name comments
- `AtlasLootClassic\Data\Token.lua`: tier tokens → pieces, quest-reward items, Sunmote upgrades
- `AtlasLootClassic_Crafting\data-tbc.lua`, `AtlasLootClassic\Data\Profession.lua`, `AtlasLootClassic\Data\Recipe.lua`: crafted items, required skill, reagents
- `AtlasLootClassic_Factions\data-tbc.lua`: reputation rewards and standings
- `AtlasLootClassic_Collections\data-tbc.lua`, `AtlasLootClassic\Data\VendorPrice.lua`: Badge of Justice vendors and costs
- `AtlasLootClassic_PvP\data-tbc.lua`, `AtlasLootClassic\Data\ItemSet.lua`: PvP gear
- `AtlasLootClassic\Data\Requirements.lua`: class bit masks (its CLASSIC and BCC tables), the source of `Data\ItemClasses.lua`
- `AtlasLootClassic_Data\source-tbc.lua`: used as a cross-check
- For `Data\Atlas.lua`: the same Crafting, Factions, PvP and Collections tables in full (not only gear), the Tier 4-6 set tables of `AtlasLootClassic_DungeonsAndRaids\data-tbc.lua`, and `ItemSet.lua` for the set pieces

### LoonBestInSlot BiS lists (shipped since 1.2.0)

- Up to 1.1.0 DisasterZone shipped no LoonBestInSlot data: the **Suggest** button in My BiS read that addon live and was hidden for members who didn't have it. Since 1.2.0 the lists are shipped, so every member gets the same suggestions.
- `Data\BiSLists.lua` is generated by `dev/generators/gen_bis_lists.py` from the guide files of **LoonBestInSlot 1.0.9** (`Guides\<Class><Spec>.lua`, 25 guides). Those files name wowhead's TBC class guides as their own source.
- What is taken: for phases 1-5, each guide's item IDs, the slot, the order, and whether the guide marks the item "BIS". Not taken: any LoonBestInSlot code, its gem and enchant lists, its pre-raid lists, its item-source database or texts.
- **LoonBestInSlot has no license file**, so there is no written permission to reuse its lists. Inside the guild this is the same common practice as the other numeric data here. **Before any public release (CurseForge, Wago, GitHub), ask LoonBestInSlot's author, or remove `Data\BiSLists.lua`** (Suggest then says the lists are missing; nothing else depends on the file).
- At generation time, LoonBestInSlot's item-source table was also read to cross-check the AtlasLoot data.
- **One caveat:** 10 item IDs in `Data\Items.lua` name LoonBestInSlot in their `[supplement: ...]` comment. The generator added them because LoonBestInSlot and Questie both list them as drops:
  - the other-faction Magtheridon's Head, 32386 (also listed by LootReserve);
  - nine "of the Forgotten" bracer/belt/boot tokens under Eredar Twins, 34848 and 34851–34858. The data file marks these as `unconfirmed` for that boss.

  These are bare item IDs, not LoonBestInSlot content. If you want the data to depend on AtlasLoot alone, remove these 10 IDs and regenerate.

### GPL terms may apply when DisasterZone is redistributed

`Data\Items.lua`, `Data\Dungeons.lua`, `Data\Sources.lua`, `Data\ItemClasses.lua` and `Data\Atlas.lua` are derived from GPL v2 data. If DisasterZone is redistributed (posted publicly, uploaded to CurseForge, Wago or GitHub, or handed to people outside the guild), the **GPL v2 may apply to those files, and possibly to the addon as a whole** when shipped with them. That can mean:

- including a copy of the GPL v2 text (copy `AtlasLootClassic\LICENSE`; DisasterZone does not ship one yet);
- keeping the attribution in this file and in the data file headers;
- giving recipients the same rights under the GPL, and adding no further restrictions;
- making the source available. The Lua files are the source, but the generator scripts aren't included.

Whether bare game IDs and drop tables are protected by copyright at all is unclear. Before a public release, decide whether to accept the GPL for DisasterZone, or to ship without these files and generate them locally.

The same caution applies to the IDs taken from DBM ("All Rights Reserved") and from addons with no license file (Questie, LootReserve). What was taken is numeric game data, not code.

## 5. Strategy board raid maps and boss portraits

**Raid maps (`Media\Maps\*.blp`, listed in `Data\RaidMaps.lua`).** These are Blizzard Entertainment's own floor maps of the TBC raids (the in-game world map art of later clients). The Anniversary client has none. They were downloaded from wowhead's image server (wow.zamimg.com, `maps/enus/original/<zone>-<floor>.jpg`) and converted to DXT1 BLP textures by `dev/generators/gen_raid_maps.py`. They are © Blizzard Entertainment. They are not openly licensed: they ship here the same way addons commonly reuse game art, for use inside World of Warcraft only. Don't redistribute them outside that context. `dev/art/blizzard_maps/` in the dev kit keeps the downloaded originals.

**Boss abilities (`Data\BossAbilities.lua`).** Only numbers: spell IDs from the installed DBM-Raids-BC modules, and for each one whether players can remove it (its dispel type: Magic, Curse, Poison, Disease, Enrage) as listed on wowhead.com. Names and icons come from the game client.

**Boss portraits (`Data\BossPortraits.lua`).** Only numbers and names: creature IDs from Questie's database (GPL-3.0) and creature display IDs as listed on wowhead.com. The portraits themselves are drawn by the game client from its own models; no Blizzard art is shipped.

The shapes of the strategy board (`Media\Strategy\disc.tga`, `ring.tga`, `arrow.tga`) are drawn by `dev/generators/gen_strategy_media.py`.
